Desired functions to implement for a portable Xlisp-Stat desktop calculator application:

1. Paschen's Formula for arcing between a gap in air. 
   - Name: paschen-breakdown
   - Variables: atomospheric pressure - kilo-Pascals
     		gap distance          - meters
   - Output:    breakdown voltage     - Volts

2. Metal-Insulator-Metal Capacitor Dielectric Constant calculation:
   - Name: calculate-dielectric-constant
   - Variables: capacitance - Farads
     		thickness   - meters
   		area        - meters squared
   - Output: dielectric constant - unitless

3. Metal-Insulator-Metal Capacitor Dielectric Constant calculation from two capacitors in series:
   - Name: calculate-dielectric-constnat-capseries
   - Variables: capacitance - Farads
     		thickness 1 - meters
